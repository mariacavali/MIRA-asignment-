# Mira V3 — Current State

## Purpose

**Mira V3 is the Recognition Engine.** It is designed to help a person recognise patterns in who they already are, understand their current chapter, and translate that recognition into an authentic personal brand and visual expression. It is deliberately not predictive, diagnostic, or prescriptive; the user’s own words remain the highest authority. [1]

> “Recognition always comes before strategy.” — *Mira V3 Constitution* [1]

## User Journey

| Journey movement | What the user does | What Mira produces or protects |
|---|---|---|
| Entry and birth calibration | Provides birth name, date, place, and either a birth time or an explicit “unknown” choice. | Retains a private calibration context. The entire birth stage is required, although birth time may be unknown. [1] |
| Conversation One | Takes part in a deep-listening conversation. | Collects evidence without presenting conclusions. [1] |
| Hidden Recognition Layer | Continues through an adaptive second conversation. | Uses conversational patterns as the primary evidence source, with private birth intelligence only as supporting context. [1] |
| Brand Soul File | Reviews Mira’s complete recognition synthesis. | Creates the single complete synthesis of recognition. [1] |
| Confirmation | Clarifies or edits the Brand Soul File. | Prevents visual translation until the user confirms that the synthesis feels true. [1] |
| Visual Translation | May upload image references after confirmation. | Translates the confirmed identity into visual expression. [1] |
| Final outputs | Receives three documents. | Produces the Brand Soul File, Brand Expression Guide, and Shoot Mood Board—no additional report types. [1] |

## Intelligence and Integration Model

V3 is **conversation-led**. Its Recognition Engine works from conversation, language patterns, optional uploaded images, and a private Recognition Layer. Important observations need support from more than one source; when evidence is insufficient, the correct action is another question rather than a claim. [1]

The private birth-intelligence integration is **Dakidarts**, a numerology provider. In the V3 experience, provider names, calculations, numbers, and numerology terminology must never be surfaced to the user. The integration is intended to improve adaptive questioning and confidence in pattern recognition, not to override the person’s own language. [1]

There is **no active Human Design API** in the current V3 implementation. If Human Design is reintroduced, it should be treated as a separately approved product and privacy decision—not as a silent substitute for the existing V3 evidence model.

## What V3 Delivers

| Document | Primary role |
|---|---|
| **Brand Soul File** | The confirmed recognition synthesis: current chapter, strengths, shadows, zone of genius, decision compass, natural contribution, and growth edge. [1] |
| **Brand Expression Guide** | Translation into positioning, audience, messaging, voice, visual language, and creative direction. [1] |
| **Shoot Mood Board** | A visual world intended to prepare the person emotionally and visually for a future shoot. [1] |

## Product Role

V3 answers the deeper question: **“What is true about me, and what kind of brand expression follows from that?”** It is the stronger product when the user needs recognition, language, meaning, and a considered brand foundation before making visual campaign choices.

## Useful Next Decisions for V3

The most practical V3 work is stability and quality refinement: ensuring the three confirmed documents remain coherent, reviewing the tone of Conversation Two, and maintaining the private boundary around optional birth intelligence. The next product-level decision is whether a confirmed V3 Brand Soul File should become an intentional handoff into V4 rather than asking the user to repeat identity work.
